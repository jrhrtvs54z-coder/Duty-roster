# Alert Duty Roster

Plan your squadron's alert duty days on your phone. It shows who is on alert, the watch bill, who enters late, who leaves early and who rests. Back-to-back days and night watches are spread fairly.

**Open it:** https://jrhrtvs54z-coder.github.io/Duty-roster/

## Install

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. Then open Duty Roster from your home screen.
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or Install app).

On iPhone, the home-screen app keeps its own data, separate from Safari. Install it first, then set it up or restore a backup inside the app.

## Bring in a roster you already have

On the first screen, tap **Restore a backup** and choose the backup file, or paste its text. Later you can do the same from **Setup → Restore**.

## Privacy

There's no account and no server. Everything you enter stays on this phone, inside the app.
Removing the app from your home screen deletes that data, so use **Setup → Back up now** once a month and keep the file somewhere safe, like iCloud Drive or Google Drive.
This repository holds only the app's code. It has no names, dates or rosters.

## Getting the roster to the pilots

Pilots don't need the app. On any duty day, tap **Copy for WhatsApp** and paste it in the group. **Month → Copy for Excel** copies the whole month.

## How it plans

- Your squadron and the other squadron alternate 24-hour alert days. Swap days are supported.
- Each shift has 4 pairs on watches you set, and every pair has a flight lead.
- Back-to-back duty days, night watches and watch hours are spread fairly. A third day in a row happens only when there's no other way.
- Any duty day a pilot isn't on alert is a rest day. You can keep a pilot off alert for a planned rest, one day or several in a row.
- Late entry and early leave rotate fairly within the daily limits. Back-to-back pilots get them first.
- Pilots marked "Fewer watch hrs" (for example the CO and XO) get the shortest watches.
- Leave, courses and deployments are worked around automatically.
